How We Teach

Our Teaching Method

✦

A modern, student-centred approach rooted in classical Islamic scholarship

Online One-on-One

Live, interactive sessions via Zoom, Google Meet, or Skype. Every lesson is personalised; the teacher focuses entirely on your pace, pronunciation, and progress. No group distractions.

  • HD video & audio sessions
  • Screen share for Quran text
  • Session recordings available

Flexible Schedule

Classes available 7 days a week, 8 AM to 10 PM Pakistan Standard Time. Choose slots that fit your timezone and daily routine: morning, afternoon or evening.

  • 5 days / week sessions
  • 30 or 60 minute classes
  • Easy rescheduling policy

Certified Curriculum

Structured syllabus aligned with Al-Huda International standards. Progress is tracked, tested, and certified at each milestone. Parents receive regular progress reports.

  • Monthly progress reports
  • Milestone certificates
  • WhatsApp parent updates
